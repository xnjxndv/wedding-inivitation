How to Reach
From Kochi & Trivandrum

From Kochi

~40 km · 1–1½ hrs by road
Head south on NH 66
  1. Ernakulam · Vyttila HubStart point in the city
  2. Kundannoor JunctionJoin NH 66 towards Alappuzha
  3. Aroor BridgeCross the backwaters into Alappuzha district
  4. ThuravoorPass Thuravoor railway station — venue is ~7 km ahead
  5. Pattanakkad → UzhuvaContinue towards Cherthala — venue is ~1–2 km past Pattanakkad
  6. Uzhuva · VenueUzhuva Panchajanyam Auditorium

By train: Ernakulam Jn → Cherthala (coastal line), about 45 min, then ~4 km by auto / taxi.

From the airport: Cochin Airport → Vyttila → NH 66 south, about 1½–2 hrs.

~170 km · 4–5 hrs by road
Head north on NH 66
  1. ThiruvananthapuramStart point in the city
  2. Kollam → KayamkulamContinue north on NH 66
  3. AlappuzhaBackwater town — about 1 hr more to go
  4. CherthalaLandmark: Cherthala railway station — venue is ~4 km north
  5. Uzhuva · VenueUzhuva Panchajanyam Auditorium

By train (recommended): Trivandrum Central → Cherthala via Alappuzha, about 3½–4 hrs. Then a short auto / taxi ride.
