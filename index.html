Where we celebrate

Uzhuva Panchajanyam Auditorium

Uzhuva, Pattanakkad, Cherthala, Alappuzha District, Kerala

  • Landmark: Puthiyakavu Devi TempleThe auditorium is right next to the temple, just off NH 66 at Uzhuva — between Cherthala (south) and Thuravoor (north).
  • Nearest railway stationCherthala (SRTL) — about 4 km · Thuravoor (TUVR) — about 7 km

How to Reach
From Kochi & Trivandrum

From Kochi

~40 km · 1–1½ hrs by road
Head south on NH 66
  1. Ernakulam · Vyttila HubStart point in the city
  2. Kundannoor JunctionJoin NH 66 towards Alappuzha
  3. Aroor BridgeCross the backwaters into Alappuzha district
  4. ThuravoorPass Thuravoor railway station — venue is ~7 km ahead
  5. Puthiyakavu Devi Temple~1–2 km past Pattanakkad on NH 66 — the venue is right next to the temple
  6. Uzhuva · VenueUzhuva Panchajanyam Auditorium

By train: Ernakulam Jn → Cherthala (coastal line), about 45 min, then ~4 km by auto / taxi.

From the airport: Cochin Airport → Vyttila → NH 66 south, about 1½–2 hrs.
